# OnboardPlan — Feature pillars

## Core engine
- Builds 30/60/90 day onboarding plans by role
- Role-tuned goals, check-ins, and owners
- First-week checklist for concrete early days

## Workflow & output
- Managers and People Ops without a full L&D team
- One shared ramp plan for hire and manager
- Editable draft — not HR policy

## Trust & control
- Planning template only — not legal advice
- No fabricated retention-ROI claims
- Demo mode without a live API key
